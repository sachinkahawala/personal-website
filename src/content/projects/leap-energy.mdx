## Data

Energy consumption, generation, and emissions data across multiple campuses.

## Objective

Forecast energy usage and optimise building operations in real time.

## Outcomes

- Developed and deployed a cloud-based analytics platform for forecasting and scenario modelling.
- Enabled optimisation of energy consumption and supported sustainability initiatives across the university's campuses.

## Tech

Azure, Databricks, XGBoost, PyTorch, MLFlow.
