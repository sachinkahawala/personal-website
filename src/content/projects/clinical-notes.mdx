## Data

De-identified clinical transcripts and medical records.

## Objective

Extract structured clinical information from doctor-patient conversations to support documentation.

## Outcomes

- Built an NLP/LLM-based system to extract key medical attributes and generate structured notes.
- Designed an evaluation pipeline combining rule-based and model-based methods to monitor accuracy and surface failure modes.

## Tech

Python, LangChain, Hugging Face, Azure OpenAI, DeepEval.
