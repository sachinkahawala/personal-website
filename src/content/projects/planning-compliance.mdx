## Data

Statutory planning schemes, council policies, developer submissions, and prior approvals across multiple Australian jurisdictions.

## Objective

Automate the assessment of development proposals against multi-jurisdiction planning controls and generate draft compliance reports with traceable evidence.

## Outcomes

- Designed and shipped a production AI platform that processes large planning and policy documents, identifies compliance gaps, and produces structured, report-ready outputs with cited evidence.
- Modelled human assessment workflows through multi-step pipelines, enabling consistent analysis at scale and reducing manual review time by **>70%**.
- Built an evaluation framework combining automated and human-in-the-loop assessment to ensure accuracy, citation grounding, and reliability in production.
- Instrumented the pipeline for usage, cost, and latency tracking to drive ongoing performance optimisation.

## Tech

Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Search, FastAPI, React, Azure Container Apps, Cosmos DB, Blob Storage, Azure Service Bus, Application Insights, LangGraph, DeepEval.
